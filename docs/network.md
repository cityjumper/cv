## MathWorks

High-level relationships built over nearly a decade at MathWorks — spanning academic leadership worldwide (from [driving the global Academic strategy](experience.md#mathworks)) and the MATLAB/Simulink partner ecosystem, including the Arduino collaboration behind their Engineering Kit.

## Open Source

Strong open-source connections, built one conversation at a time through [Inspiring Computing and Tangible Computing](podcast.md) — guests have included Ralf Gommers (Director, Quansight Labs; NumPy), Wolf Vollprecht (Co-founder, Prefix.dev; Pixi), Ritchie Vink (author, Polars), Tim Besard (JuliaHub), and dozens more across the Python, Julia, and Rust ecosystems.

## NumFOCUS & PyData

Helped drive NumFOCUS's growth within the Julia community and across [PyData events](events.md#pydata-eindhoven), and organized the first-ever NumFOCUS PyData European Organizer Workshop.
